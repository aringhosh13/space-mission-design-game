# Real Earth return: Artemis I reference

After completing all twelve First Light objectives, the report offers **REAL EARTH RETURN: ARTEMIS I**.
This opens a skippable, captioned historical explanation. It does not extend the fictional ship's route,
change the report, deliver Mars material to Earth or claim that the Moon/Mars game expedition happened.
There is no new player-controlled Earth landing or atmospheric-flight simulation.

## Facts shown and exact NASA sources

The reference is the **uncrewed Artemis I mission, 11 December 2022**:

- Orion separated its crew module from the service module before entry. The capsule used skip entry;
  atmospheric entry preceded staged parachute descent.
  [NASA: crew module separation and entry sequence](https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/artemis/2022/12/11/artemis-i-flight-day-26-orion-crew-module-separation-from-service-module-complete/)
- Orion splashed down in the Pacific Ocean and was recovered for inspection after its lunar mission.
  [NASA: Artemis I splashdown and recovery](https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/artemis/2022/12/11/artemis-i-flight-day-26-orion-splashes-down-concluding-historic-artemis-i-mission/)

Sources directly checked on 2026-11-15. Captions paraphrase these accounts; no footage, logos or artwork is
copied. The capsule, service module, parachutes, Earth and ocean are illustrative procedural models. Three
short shots show separation, a protected-entry close-up and the main-parachute/splashdown phase. These are
not engineering replicas, exact trajectories, atmospheric models or a complete deployment timeline. The
entry shot does not purport to animate the full skip manoeuvre. Distances and timing are illustrative.

Why no claim of a real Mars-to-Earth flight? NASA's
[Mars Sample Return page](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-sample-return/) describes a proposed
multi-mission campaign for returning Martian samples, not an accomplished flight of our game's reusable
ship. The real lunar-return reference is kept distinct from the fictional expedition.

## In-game explanations

First Light retains its problem briefing, candidate-route presentation with calculated energy/slope values,
Mission Control checkpoint instructions and actual-results closing film. All have readable captions and
can be skipped where appropriate. Replay Briefing is available in the planning lab. The historical film is
optional and can be replayed from the completed report; Space/Enter/Escape or its button returns to it.

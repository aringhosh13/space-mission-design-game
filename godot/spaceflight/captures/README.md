# First Light captures

Offscreen renders of the real campaign scenes (Godot 4.7.2 OpenGL 3, Mesa llvmpipe under Xvfb, 1280×720),
made by Claude on 2026-11-15. `01` and `06` come from the driven route (`tests/spaceflight/campaign_route.gd`
with `--capture-dir`). The others come from a presentation script that places the rover for framing.
Generated by the game's own procedural rendering and Codex's LOLA-derived Moon terrain. No external imagery.
`.gdignore` keeps Godot from importing or exporting this folder.

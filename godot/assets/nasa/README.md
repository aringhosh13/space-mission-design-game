# NASA-published science images

These three files are educational reference images in **Science [F2]**. They are not generated artwork,
gameplay screenshots, terrain heightmaps or NASA-observed placements of our fictional resource sites.
They were retrieved on **2026-11-15**, inspected visually and bundled unchanged from the published asset
links below. Godot scales them to fit without cropping. `manifest.json` records SHA-256 and exact URLs.

## moon-polar-ice.jpg

- Credit: **NASA**. M³ was NASA's Moon Mineralogy Mapper aboard ISRO's Chandrayaan-1.
- [NASA/JPL: Ice Confirmed at the Moon's Poles](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/ice-confirmed-at-the-moons-poles/), 20 August 2018.
- [Published JPL CDN asset](https://d2pn8kiwq2w21t.cloudfront.net/images/imagesmoon20180820elphic20180820-16.width-1280.jpg).
- Polar ice reference only. M³ supplies this image; the separate LOLA dataset supplies our lunar elevations.

## jezero-delta.jpg

- Credit: **NASA/JPL-Caltech/LANL/CNES/CNRS/ASU/MSSS**.
- [NASA: My Favorite Martian Image: Jezero Crater's Delta Scarp](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/my-favorite-martian-image-jezero-craters-delta-scarp/).
- [Published annotated PIA24683 asset](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/1-pia24683a-annotated-1041.jpg).
- This is NASA's annotated SuperCam RMI mosaic, not an unprocessed photograph. The original arrows and
  labels are retained. It illustrates geological investigation; the separate MOLA product supplies elevations.

## curiosity-dust-comparison.jpg

- Credit: **NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS**.
- [NASA Astrobiology: Forecasting Martian Dust Storms](https://astrobiology.nasa.gov/news/forecasting-martian-dust-storms/), 27 July 2018.
- [Published PIA22330 comparison asset](https://astrobiology.nasa.gov/uploads/filer_public_thumbnails/filer_public/9e/5a/9e5a5cff-71db-4a7f-827b-c7c65c37b36e/21928_pia22330-web.jpg__1240x510_q85_subject_location-800%2C366_subsampling-2.jpg).
- The comparison comes from Curiosity, which uses radioisotope power. The text separately explains
  Opportunity's solar-power vulnerability. These are historical observations, not our game's weather data.
